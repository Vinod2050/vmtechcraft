Frequently asked questions

Can a web developer in India work with US businesses?

Yes. India is 9½–13½ hours ahead of US time zones. I reply to overnight messages before your day starts, and schedule calls in your morning (my evening) for the East Coast or by email and async video for the West Coast. Everything else runs over email, WhatsApp and a private preview link where you can see progress as it happens. I already build for US clients, including Aspen Privé, a private-stays brand in Aspen, Colorado, and KOS, a plant-based nutrition store.

Can my online store accept Stripe and PayPal?

Yes. Depending on your platform and business account, I can connect Stripe, PayPal, Square, Shopify Payments and Apple Pay and Google Pay, along with shipping, tax settings and order emails.

Will my website follow privacy rules in the USA?

Cookie notices and privacy pages set up for US state privacy laws such as the CCPA in California, and accessibility built to WCAG 2.2 guidelines, which matters for ADA-related risk. I’m a developer, not a lawyer, so the wording of your privacy policy should come from your legal adviser or a trusted policy generator.

What language will my website be in?

US English spelling and conventions throughout: dollar pricing, US date and phone formats, and copy written for American customers.

Do you work as a white-label developer for US agencies?

Yes. Agencies can send me designs or briefs and I build the site under their brand, with no contact with their client unless they want it. Work is delivered to your staging server or Git repository.
