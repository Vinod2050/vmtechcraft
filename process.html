Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer here? Message me on WhatsApp and you'll hear back the same day.

How long does a project take?

A typical business website takes 3–4 weeks: design in week 1, build in weeks 2–3, then launch. Online stores and web applications take longer, and you get a clear timeline together with your quote.

How is the price decided?

After a free discovery call you get a fixed quote in writing within 24 hours, so you know the full cost before anything starts.

Will I see the design before you write code?

Yes. You get clickable Figma designs of the key screens. You get up to 2 rounds of changes, and only then does the build begin.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I work remotely with clients in India, the US, the UK and Australia, over WhatsApp, email and calls.

What happens after launch?

You get a short training video and 30 days of free fixes. After that, monthly maintenance is available: updates, backups, speed and SEO checks, and quick edits.
