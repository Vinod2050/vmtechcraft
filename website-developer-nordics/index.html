Working together

One developer, several markets

The Nordic countries combine high budgets, a real shortage of developers and excellent English, which makes them some of the easiest markets in Europe to work with remotely. Most businesses there are digital-first and expect clear communication and well-documented work.

The region shares a lot, from strict GDPR cookie consent to the popularity of Klarna, but each country has its own payment apps and language. Here’s what I set up for each.

SwedenNorwayDenmarkFinland

Sweden · Stockholm · Gothenburg · Malmö

Web developer for Swedish businesses

Sweden has a serious developer shortage, high budgets and excellent English, which makes it one of the easiest European markets to work with remotely.

  • Time zone: Sweden is 3½–4½ hours behind India. Your morning is my early afternoon, so we share most of your working day.
  • Language: English or Swedish websites (or both). You or your translator supply the Swedish text. Prices in SEK or euros.
  • Payments: Klarna, Swish, Stripe, PayPal and cards and Apple Pay.
  • Privacy: Cookie consent and privacy pages set up for the GDPR, with analytics that only load after consent.
  • I build for: startups and SaaS products, e-commerce brands, consultants and agencies and clinics and therapists.

Norway · Oslo · Bergen · Trondheim

Web developer for Norwegian businesses

Norwegian businesses have high budgets, very high local developer rates and excellent English, so outsourcing to a reliable developer abroad is a practical way to launch faster for less.

  • Time zone: Norway is 3½–4½ hours behind India. Your morning is my early afternoon, so we share most of your working day.
  • Language: English or Norwegian websites (or both). You or your translator supply the Norwegian text. Prices in NOK or euros.
  • Payments: Vipps MobilePay, Klarna, Stripe, PayPal and cards and Apple Pay.
  • Privacy: Cookie consent and privacy pages set up for the GDPR (which applies in Norway through the EEA), with analytics that only load after consent.
  • I build for: tourism and outdoor activities, startups and SaaS products, maritime and energy suppliers and e-commerce.

Denmark · Copenhagen · Aarhus · Odense

Web developer for Danish businesses

Danish businesses are digital-first, comfortable in English and short of developers, which makes remote collaboration easy and cost-effective.

  • Time zone: Denmark is 3½–4½ hours behind India. Your morning is my early afternoon, so we share most of your working day.
  • Language: English or Danish websites (or both). You or your translator supply the Danish text. Prices in DKK or euros.
  • Payments: Vipps MobilePay, Dankort via QuickPay, Stripe, Klarna and cards and Apple Pay.
  • Privacy: Cookie consent and privacy pages set up for the GDPR and Danish cookie rules, with analytics that only load after consent.
  • I build for: startups and SaaS products, design and furniture brands, e-commerce and consultants.

Finland · Helsinki · Espoo · Tampere

Web developer for Finnish businesses

Finland’s tech-savvy businesses face a real developer shortage, and most are comfortable working in English with remote partners.

  • Time zone: Finland is 2½–3½ hours behind India. Your morning is my late morning, so we share almost the entire working day.
  • Language: English or Finnish websites (Swedish too). You or your translator supply the translations. Prices in euros.
  • Payments: Paytrail, Vipps MobilePay, Klarna, Stripe and cards and Apple Pay.
  • Privacy: Cookie consent and privacy pages set up for the GDPR, with analytics that only load after consent.
  • I build for: startups and gaming companies, saaS products, e-commerce and consultants.

Frequently asked questions

More about how a project works

Will my website handle GDPR cookie consent?

Yes. Cookie consent is set up so analytics and marketing tags only load after consent, with a privacy page. The legal wording should come from your adviser or a trusted policy generator.

Can you connect Swish, Vipps, MobilePay or Klarna?

Yes, depending on your platform and business account. Each country section on this page lists the usual options.

Can the site be in Swedish, Norwegian, Danish or Finnish?

Yes. I build multilingual sites with a language switcher. You or your translator supply the translated text; we communicate in English.

How do time zones work?

The Nordics are 2½–4½ hours behind India, so your morning is my early afternoon and we share most of your working day.
