Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in India?

It ranges widely, from template sites to custom agency builds. What you pay depends on pages, features, content and integrations. I give a fixed quote in rupees after a free call, so you know the full cost upfront.

How do I hire an affordable freelance web developer in India?

Look for three things: a fixed quote in writing, live projects you can open and check, and your domain and code registered in your name. That is how I work, delivering agency-quality websites and software at a fair, fixed price. See my live client projects before you decide.

Is it safe to hire a freelance developer in another city?

Yes, when the process is clear: a written quote, designs approved before coding, a live preview link while the site is built, and domain and hosting registered in your name, so you always stay in control.

Do you work with startups?

Yes. I build MVPs, landing pages and product websites for founders. See custom web application development.

Can my website be in Hindi or another Indian language?

Yes. Websites can be built in English plus Hindi, Marathi or another language with a language switch, using translated content that you provide or approve.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical business website takes 3–4 weeks from the first call to launch. Online stores and custom software take longer, and you get a timeline with your quote.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes 30 days of free fixes, and monthly maintenance plans are available after that.
