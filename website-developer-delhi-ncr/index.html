One region, many markets

Delhi NCR is really several business markets side by side. Gurugram has corporate offices, SaaS companies and funded startups along Cyber City and Golf Course Road. Noida has IT companies, electronics makers and a growing number of D2C brands. Delhi itself runs on trade: wholesale markets in Chandni Chowk, Karol Bagh and Sadar Bazar, and manufacturers and exporters in Okhla, Mayapuri and Bawana.

Competition for customers here is intense, and buyers compare you online before they call. I build websites and software that hold up to that comparison: fast D2C stores, company websites that win export and B2B buyers, MVPs for founders, and billing and dealer software for traders, all with a fixed quote agreed before work starts.
